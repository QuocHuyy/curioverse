Hi, I'm Huy. This is the first post on Curioverse, the place where I tell stories, share my work and keep a record of how I grow.

I already have GitHub, a few repositories and a rather loud profile page. But a repository only shows *what code I wrote*. It cannot tell you why I started, where I stumbled, or what I understood after each project. Curioverse exists to fill that gap.

## Why "Curioverse"?

**Curio**sity + uni**verse**: a universe of curiosity. I like to think of every post as a star. Alone, it is a small dot of light. But once posts connect through shared ideas they form constellations, and from far away you get a whole sky.

<Sidenote>Try the **Day / Night** button at the top. By day the site is a notebook page; by night it turns into a starry sky.</Sidenote>

So every post here has its **own constellation**, generated from the post's name, and no two are the same. For the big picture, open the [Star map](/en/universe), where every post is drawn as part of one graph.

## What's here?

- **[Stories](/en/stories)**: experiences, reflections and look-backs. Written slowly and honestly.
- **[Work](/en/work)**: projects, competitions and research. Each one covers the problem, my approach, the outcome and what I learned.
- **[Journey](/en/journey)**: a timeline of the milestones that brought me here.
- **[Notes](/en/notes)**: a knowledge garden of short notes that grow over time.

## Allowed to publish as a "nebula"

My biggest fear in writing is waiting until a piece is *perfect*, and then never publishing it. So every note has a **maturity**:

| Glyph | Maturity | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| · | Nebula | A raw, fuzzy idea. Allowed to be wrong. |
| ✧ | Protostar | Taking shape, still being refined. |
| ✦ | Star | Mature, reliable and quotable. |

A note can start as a nebula and slowly become a star as I understand more. I think that is how I learn too: not in a straight line, but getting brighter over time.

## An invitation

If curiosity brought you here, welcome. You can follow along via [RSS](/en/rss.xml), check the [Now](/en/now) page to see what I'm up to, or read the [About](/en/about) page.

This universe only has a few stars so far. It will keep expanding.
