<h2 id="colophon">How this site is built</h2>

Curioverse is a static site built with [Astro](https://astro.build). Every post is a Markdown/MDX file in the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/QuocHuyy/curioverse), and every push redeploys the site through GitHub Actions.

- **Look**: pixel + anime. Icons, constellations and the Curio mascot are all drawn pixel by pixel.
- **Type**: Handjet (pixel headings), M PLUS Rounded 1c (body), VT323 (small HUD labels), JetBrains Mono (code). All four cover Vietnamese diacritics.
- **Star map**: a graph of all posts, laid out with `d3-force` at build time and drawn as SVG.
- **A constellation for every post**: random, but fixed by the post's name.
- **Search**: Pagefind. **Share images**: Satori and sharp. **Math**: KaTeX.
- **Day/Night**: follows your system, or pick one at the top.

Most writing here is in Vietnamese; selected posts have English versions.
